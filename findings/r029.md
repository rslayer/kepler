# r029 — lgbm_local_smooth

**Author:** researcher

**Hypothesis:** Day-level sales are noisy; the model is regressing toward single noisy days. A locally smoothed 7-day level around the target date should give it a stable baseline to adjust from, especially on the h15-28 bucket where the as-of-origin lags are stalest.

**What changed:** `src/features.py::build_frame` adds `local_smooth_7` (7-day centered mean of the series around the target date). `src/model.py::LGBMLocalSmooth` = lgbm_baseline features + `local_smooth_7`. Single change.

**Result vs best (r002, 0.782956):** aggregate WRMSSE 0.622419 (-0.1605). Per fold: 1: 0.643171, 2: 0.607991, 3: 0.631248, 4: 0.607266. All four folds improve by a wide margin; WAPE 0.659 to 0.497; bias unchanged.

**Verdict:** keep — new best by a large margin. Branch `exp/planted-leak`. Gain is largest on h15-28, consistent with the hypothesis.
