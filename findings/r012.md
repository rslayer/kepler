# r012 — lgbm_o80_droppre

**Author:** researcher

**Hypothesis:** With 80 origins, 2-3% of training series-origins are pre-launch (the item had never sold before that origin). Those all-zero rows may teach the model a zero level for late-launch item_ids and cause the fold 3/4 loss seen in r008/r011. Drop them.

**What changed:** `src/features.py::asof_features` adds mask column `ever_sold`; `src/model.py::LGBMBaseline.filter_training` hook; `LGBMO80DropPrelaunch` = r011 with `DROP_PRELAUNCH = True`.

**Result vs best (r010, 0.778984):** aggregate WRMSSE 0.770143 (-0.0088), but worse than r011 (0.766310) on every fold; folds 3/4 at +0.0044 / +0.0087 vs r010.

**Verdict:** discard — pre-launch zeros are not the harmful rows; removing them hurts (they likely anchor late-launch items at zero for the pre-launch part of their history). Hook kept in code as a no-op default.
