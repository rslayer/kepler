(`lgbm_xmas0_tgd_r3_ye`) adds ("Christmas", (-1, +1)) and ("NewYear", (-1, 0)). Folds 1 and

The multipliers are below 1 on the year-end days on balance and move the forecast in the
right direction for the typical series (WAPE) but not for the dollar-weighted squared error:
a department-wide ratio is the wrong resolution for the big FOODS/HOBBIES series that dominate
WRMSSE around Christmas (some of which spike on 24 and 31 December rather than dip). Another
instance of the WAPE-better / WRMSSE-neutral pattern [LESSONS r015, r026, r042]. Thanksgiving
worked because its four-day dip is broad across departments; the year-end days are not.
