## Mechanism (why the lever died)
Finer horizon buckets thin the per-model training data (mh28 = 1/28 the rows per model). WRMSSE
is won on the sparse, episodic HOLIDAY spikes; a per-day bucket model has almost no holiday
examples for its single horizon-day, whereas the champion's weekly bucket pools ~7 days of
holiday-adjacent signal. So finer buckets do the OPPOSITE of the goal: ~neutral on calm months,
collapse on holidays. The champion's 4 weekly buckets is at/beyond the sweet spot for bucket
count. To specialise finer horizons one would need finer target-relative LAGS (a feature lever,
not bucket count), not thinner data — a separate hypothesis.
