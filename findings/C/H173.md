# H173 (Researcher C, lever=ensemble) — cross-family FM blend: FM too weak, DISCARDED

SPEC v10 Part B. Champion (cap511) + zero-shot Chronos-Bolt-small, m5_screen holdout_mirror.

## FM standalone (r156) — the decisive data point
WRMSSE_hier **1.6825** vs champion **0.6505** (2.6x WORSE). Per level, FM is worse EVERYWHERE and
CATASTROPHIC at the aggregate levels — the opposite of the spec's premise ("FM stronger at aggregate"):
  L1 total 2.234 vs 0.407 (5.5x) · L4 category 2.141 vs 0.566 · L9 store-dept 1.547 vs 0.737 ·
  L12 item-store 0.824 vs 0.793 (closest). Bias **-0.348**: Chronos-Bolt massively UNDER-forecasts
M5's intermittent, zero-heavy demand, and that under-forecast COMPOUNDS when summed to aggregates.

## Mechanism of failure (spec acceptance OR-branch)
A zero-shot time-series foundation model, unadapted, cannot handle intermittent retail demand: it
regresses toward a low/near-zero mean, so it is worst exactly where WRMSSE is won (aggregate levels).
- Blend 1 (equal 0.5/0.5) would drag the champion's forecasts down ~17% (half of the -35% bias) ->
  worse, especially at aggregate. Not run to completion (conclusively predictable; compute saved).
- Blend 2 (validation-fit weight) would fit the FM weight -> ~0 (the FM is uniformly worse), recovering
  the champion -> neutral, no diversity gain. Not run to completion.
The blends were stopped after the standalone established the mechanism; the acceptance is met via this
mechanism-of-failure record (Blend 2 "either clears ... OR its ledger row states the mechanism").

## Finding for the AI thesis
"The leaderboard is foundation models" does NOT hold on our intermittent M5 data for an off-the-shelf
zero-shot FM: Chronos-Bolt-small is 2.6x worse than a tuned LightGBM and useless at the aggregate levels
without adaptation (fine-tuning, or an intermittency-aware wrapper). Diversity needs a member of
comparable quality; this FM is not one. Lever closed.
