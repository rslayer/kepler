## Mechanism
The stacking idea has real signal — it improves 5/8 folds, some strongly (Mar16 +0.069, Sep +0.03). But
the validation-window weight is UNSTABLE: on Mar-2015 the closed-form weight over-committed to a member
that then generalised badly to the horizon, blowing the fold up +0.34 and dragging the mean negative.
Fitting one scalar weight on a single 28-day pre-origin window overfits that window. A robust follow-up
(shrink the weight toward 0.5, or bound it, or fit on multiple pre-origin windows) is the natural next
hypothesis — the lever is not dead, but this weight estimator is too fragile. Recorded for a future cycle.
