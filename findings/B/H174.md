# H174 (Researcher B, lever=hierarchy) — level-aware aggregate feature: right direction, wash

SPEC v10 Part C. `recipe6_calendar_l2_l3` = champion + `l3_level` (store-category aggregate rolling
level as-of-origin, top-down feature). Screened on holdout_mirror (r157) and quarterly (r158) m5_screen.

## Result — discarded (wash)
- Mirror (May, holdout-calibrated): mean gain **+0.0018**, 2/4 folds, discarded. Below the gate's 0.015.
- Quarterly: mean **+0.0020**, 5/8, discarded.

## Per-level (mirror): local effect, no net gain
Helps the MID-hierarchy levels near L3 (L4 category +0.0057, L5 dept +0.0085, L7-L9 +0.005-0.006) but
HURTS the top (L1 total -0.0049, L2 state -0.0078); item ~neutral. The aggregate signal improves the
levels around its own and degrades the total.

## Calm-month diagnostic (the spec's question)
Calm (summer/autumn) mean gain **+0.0026**, spring **+0.0076**, HOLIDAY (winter) **-0.0047** (Dec2015
-0.0116). So injecting aggregate signal DOES address the champion's calm-month weakness (helps calm and
spring) — but trades it for holiday accuracy, netting a wash. Right direction, wrong tradeoff.

## C2 and level-3-standalone (covered by prior evidence, not re-run)
- C2 ("level-3 as a blend member at aggregate levels, item pure") mathematically requires reconciling
  item forecasts to a blended aggregate — exactly the shrunk reconciliation already tested and discarded
  (H171, +0.0027 wash). Not re-run.
- The standalone level-3 forecaster is the light-aggregate model already shown to lose to bottom-up at
  m5_all (H159); the FM standalone (r156) also confirmed aggregate forecasting is hard here.

## Mechanism + follow-up
Aggregate-level signal — as a feature (C1), reconciled (H159/H171), or blended (C2) — is a WASH on this
hierarchy: the bottom-up item model already captures the aggregate level, so adding it back helps some
levels/seasons and hurts others. The one lead: C1 helps calm months but hurts holidays. A follow-up
that GATES the l3 feature OFF in holiday windows (like the christmas reconciliation gate) could keep the
calm-month gain without the holiday loss — a future hypothesis, not run this spec.
