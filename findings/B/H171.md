## Mechanism
Shrinkage worked as intended — it limited the damage of a wrong aggregate (the Christmas-gated Dec
folds are untouched; no blow-ups like full reconciliation H159 had at m5_all). But the light aggregate
model is still per-fold unreliable: it corrects some folds and mis-corrects others by similar amounts,
netting ~zero with one fold past tolerance. Reconciliation remains a wash on this hierarchy; the
bottom-up recipe already captures the aggregate level well enough that a separately-forecast aggregate
adds noise as often as signal. Lever parked; a materially better aggregate model would be the only way forward.
